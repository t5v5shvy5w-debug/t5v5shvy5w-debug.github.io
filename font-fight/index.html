How to play

Best of three rounds. Empty your rival's health bar, or have more left when the clock runs out.

Keyboard

  • or WASD move
  • Spaceor J attack. Tap it three times for a combo
  • Shiftor K dash
  • Eor L ultimate, when the meter is full
  • Escpause

Touch

  • Left thumbanywhere on the left half moves you
  • HITattack, DASH dodges, ULT fires the ultimate
  • Let goand you guard

Fighting

  • Combo.Attack, attack, attack. The third hit is a finisher: it knocks back, splats rivals into walls and breaks a guard.
  • Guard.Let go of the controls, or hold away from your rival. Guarded hits only chip, and the attacker is left open for a moment.
  • Dash.Dash straight through an attack. Time it as it lands for a PERFECT: your next hit counters for bonus damage. Dashes recharge, and a dash that ends next to your rival can be punished.
  • Ink.Shots cost ink, shown as three squares under your meter. It refills on its own. Close up, shooters shove instead.
  • Ultimate.Dealing and taking damage fills the meter. Fired raw it can be guarded or dodged. Cancel a landed hit into it and it's guaranteed.
