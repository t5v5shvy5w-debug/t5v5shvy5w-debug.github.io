HOW TO PLAY

  • Touch: left stick moves, drag right side to aim, FIRE to shoot.
  • Desktop: WASD moves, mouse aims (click to lock), click fires, R reloads, right-click aims down sights, Shift sprints, Space jumps, 1/2 swap weapons.
  • Clear each wave. Headshots kill in one hit and score more.
  • Health comes back after 5 seconds without damage.
