Chorus

Deutsch

Was hat der Weihnachtsmann für uns getan?

Bringt uns der Nikolaus Frieden ins Haus.

Er sitzt und nimmt die Ehre, die Jesus nur gehört.

Oh, ihr Christen, wen ehren wir?

Wer kehrt ein bei unser Haus, Jesus oder Nikolaus?

English

What has Saint Nicholas done for us?

He brings joy into our house.

He sits and takes the honor that belongs to Jesus alone.

Oh, you Christians, whom do we honor?

Who comes into our house, Jesus or Nicholas?

Verse 1

Deutsch

Jesus hat uns Heil gebracht,

in der ersten Weihnachtsnacht.

Er verließ die Himmelskrone,

kam zu einem Krippenthron.

Zu uns in dies Erdenland,

das hat er für uns getan.

English

Jesus brought us salvation

on the first Christmas night.

He left the crown of heaven,

came to a manger throne.

He came to us, into this world.

That is what he did for us.

Verse 2

Deutsch

Er kannte seinen Weg,

dornengekrönt, ans Kreuz gestiegen.

Dennoch kam er willig hin,

als ein armes kleines Kind,

nahm sich unserer Menschheit an,

das hat er für uns getan.

English

He knew the path before him,

crowned with thorns, he climbed the cross.

Yet he came willingly,

as a poor little child,

and he took our humanity.

That is what he did for us.

Verse 3

Deutsch

Keiner Gnade sind wir wert,

doch hat er uns Gnad' beschert.

Suchte uns, die wir verloren,

und zum Sterben ist geboren.

In die Finsternis er kam,

das hat er für uns getan.

English

We deserve no grace at all,

yet he gave us grace.

He sought us, we who were lost,

and he was born to die.

And he came into the darkness.

That is what he did for us.

Final Verse

Deutsch

Ihm gebührt die Ehre.

Folgen wir dem, der heilig führt.

Preisen wir den, der uns liebt,

oder den, den die Welt uns gibt?

Wer kehrt ein bei unser Haus, Jesus oder Nikolaus?

English

To him belongs the honor.

Let us follow the one who leads us in holiness.

Let us praise the one who loves us,

or the one the world gives us?

Who comes into our house, Jesus or Nicholas?

About the lyrics: no official lyrics are published for this song, so the German was transcribed from the recording and cross-checked three ways. The English is the Suno cover's lyrics, exactly as sung.

Two small differences between the versions: where the German says “Weihnachtsmann” (Santa Claus) the English cover says “Saint Nicholas”, and where the German says “Frieden” (peace) the English cover says “joy”. Everything else lines up verse for verse.
